# eliminating animal testing, i will do it.

a working journal, february to november 2026, on trying to build a computational model of a rat's response to drugs, so a new compound could be run through the model instead of through an animal.

it is not a paper. it is the notebook: three architectures, two dead ends, the nights i decided i was wrong, and the tables that still dont finish. i wrote it for a few friends who follow the project, so it is informal and lowercase and parts of it are addressed to them.

where it stands. the molecular band runs end to end for D2, with signaling through DARPP-32 and uncertainty propagation on the output. the striatal microcircuit runs and i do not trust it, because the connectivity underneath it is assumed. behavior, nothing. none of it is validated and most will turn out wrong.
